# Golden Journey — live wedding invitation

A self-contained, editable wedding invitation inspired by the ShubhWeds “template-3” rhythm (scratch-the-heart date, two-city journey, boarding-pass RSVP).

- **Invitation:** [index.html](index.html)
- **Editor:** [edit.html](edit.html) — fill in names, dates and wording; get a shareable link (`#data=`). Empty optional fields are hidden. Drafts are saved in the browser.

## Fonts
Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond, Great Vibes and Parisienne are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Google Fonts). Allura (OFL) is used in place of the commercial “Mussica Swash” face, which cannot be redistributed.

## Artwork
All paintings, the wax-seal monogram and ornaments are original. The reference invitation was studied for layout, typography and interaction only. Their images, video, audio and wording are not used.
